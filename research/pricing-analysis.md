## 🔍 Analysis: Is Your Price Too Cheap?

### YES — Your pricing is significantly below market.

Here's why this is a concern:

#### 1. Feature-to-Price Mismatch
Your platform includes features that competitors charge $100-$500+/month for:
- ✅ POS & Invoicing (typically $30-100/mo alone)
- ✅ Appointment Scheduling (typically $20-60/mo alone)
- ✅ Staff Management with Commission (typically $50-150/mo)
- ✅ Customer CRM & Loyalty (typically $30-80/mo)
- ✅ Apple Wallet & Google Wallet (rare — enterprise only)
- ✅ Multi-Branch Support (typically $200-500/mo)
- ✅ Financial Reports & Analytics (typically $50-150/mo)
- ✅ Digital Backups (typically $20-50/mo)

**Total value you're providing: $400-$1,000+/month worth of features**
**Your price: $19-27/month**

#### 2. Perception Risk
In the Gulf market, very low prices can signal:
- "Cheap quality" or "not professional"
- "Limited features" or "unreliable"
- "Not suitable for serious businesses"

#### 3. Sustainability Concern
At 69 QAR/month, you need ~15 paying customers to make just 1,035 QAR/month (~$285). That barely covers hosting and development costs.

#### 4. Fresha Comparison
Even Fresha, which markets itself as "free," charges:
- 20% commission on new marketplace clients
- Per-staff monthly fees (recently added)
- 1.2-1.4% + fees per payment transaction
- A busy salon pays $300-$1,000+/month in hidden fees

- 149 QAR sounds better than 150 QAR

- "Save 17% with annual billing" is compelling
