Our story

Five tools that never agreed with each other.

Every spa we sat with was running the same patchwork, and every one of them was reconciling it by hand.

A booking app for the diary. A separate till for payments. A notebook for attendance and another for fines. A spreadsheet for commission. Paper for everything nobody had got around to yet. Each one worked. None of them knew about the others.

So the owner became the integration layer. At the end of the month they sat down with four sources and a calculator and tried to produce one number they could trust — and the number they arrived at was always a little bit of a guess, because a record that lives in one place cannot be checked against anything.

Spa Suite is the answer to that specific problem. One database behind every desk, so a sale, a stamp, a commission and a drawer balance are the same event seen from different angles. Ring up a walk-in and the therapist's commission, the client's loyalty stamp, the drawer total and the day's report all move at once, because they are not separate things.

It is built in Doha, in riyals, with tax numbers per branch and receipts that print in Arabic as well as English — because those are not features you bolt onto a product designed somewhere else.

What we hold to

4

Four things we won't trade away.

These are the arguments we keep having internally, settled.

A receptionist should never need training twice

Front-of-house turns over. If a new person can't take a payment on their first shift without someone standing behind them, the software has failed — however capable it is underneath.

Every figure has to be traceable

No total that can't be opened up and read back to the transaction it came from. The drawer closes against a counted number, the audit log records who changed what, and the reports are built from the same rows the till wrote.

Your data leaves whenever you want it to

Reports export as PDFs and spreadsheets, and the whole database backs up in one click. We would rather earn the renewal each year than hold your records hostage to it.

Built for here, not localised for here

Riyals, per-branch tax numbers, Arabic on the receipt, Gulf working patterns and part-time staff on their own commission rules. Designed in, not translated on afterwards.

Who it's for

Owners who still count the drawer themselves.

Spa Suite is aimed at independent spas, salons and wellness centres — one location or several — in Qatar and across the Gulf.

The people it fits are running somewhere between three and forty staff, taking cash and card side by side, and carrying the whole operation in their head because no system was ever holding it for them. They don't need an enterprise rollout. They need the front desk and the back office to stop disagreeing.
