The current setup

Four places a spa quietly loses money.

Every one of them is a record that was never written down, or written down somewhere nobody looks.

The booking nobody wrote down

One lost booking

A client is turned away because the diary says a room is free when it isn't — or a therapist is double-booked and finds out when both clients are standing at reception.

A drawer that can't be checked

Unverifiable

Cash comes in and goes out all day with no opening float and no running record. At closing there is a number, but no way to tell whether it's the right one.

Payroll rebuilt from scratch every month

Hours, monthly

Attendance on paper, overtime in one notebook, fines in another, commission in a spreadsheet. Then somebody adds it all up by hand and hopes.

The client who came once

No second visit

No record of what they had, who did it, or when they were last in. Nothing to bring them back rather than the place two doors down.

A day inside

Thursday, from the float to the close.

Seven moments from an ordinary day. Each one is a record the system keeps so nobody has to.

08:45

The day is opened

The float goes into the drawer and the drawer is authorised. Until that happens, nothing can be rung up.

Business day

09:12

Aisha clocks in

Attendance is stamped against her name. Rotation puts her at the front of the queue for the next unassigned client.

Attendance · rotation

10:04

A walk-in takes a 60-minute deep tissue

Room 3 is free, so reception books and bills in one pass — 150 in cash, 100 on card. The receipt prints at 80mm with the branch's tax number on it.

POS · rooms

11:30

A booking arrives with no therapist on it

It sits in the unassigned queue rather than in somebody's head, until the next free slot that matches comes up.

Appointments

14:15

The laundry supplier is paid in cash

Logged against an expense category and taken off the drawer now, instead of being discovered at month end.

Expenses

18:20

Noor earns her eighth stamp

The reward unlocks, and the loyalty card already sitting in her Apple Wallet updates itself. She finds out before she reaches the door.

Loyalty · wallet pass

22:40

The day is closed

Counted cash is entered against what the system expected. The daily report prints, a database backup is written, and the day is locked.
