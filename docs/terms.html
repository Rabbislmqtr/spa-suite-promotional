What you are buying

2

A subscription to use it. Not the software itself.

You are buying a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use Spa Suite for your own business while your subscription is paid. The software, its source and its design remain ours. This is a licence rather than a sale, but it is deliberately not a cage: your data is yours (section 6), and nothing here stops you leaving with it.

What each plan includes, in riyals per month or per year:

  • Starter — one location, up to three staff accounts, POS and bookings, cash drawer and expenses, daily and monthly reports.
  • Professional — one location, unlimited staff accounts, everything in Starter plus loyalty stamp cards, wallet passes, payroll and commission, and customer feedback.
  • Multi-branch — unlimited locations, everything in Professional plus consolidated reporting, a shared customer database, a receipt header and tax number per branch, and priority support.

There is no commission on your takings and no charge per booking, on any plan. Current prices are on the pricing section of our site, and the plan you bought is the one named on your invoice.

Accounts

You decide who gets an account and which of the six roles they hold, and you are responsible for what those accounts do. Tell us promptly if a password has been shared too widely, an account is in the wrong hands, or a device with a signed-in terminal has gone missing. We can end that session from our side once you ask.

Your data

You can export your reports at any time, take a full database backup whenever you like, and have the final copy handed to you before your access ends. The privacy page describes what is stored, where it sits and how it is deleted, and forms part of these terms.

We are not responsible for losses caused by that infrastructure — a dead disk, a lapsed certificate, an expired hosting plan. We will still help you recover, and if you would rather not carry any of it, the hosted arrangement puts it on us.

If the code has been modified locally, we may not be able to reproduce a fault on our own copy. We will tell you that plainly rather than blame the modification by default.
