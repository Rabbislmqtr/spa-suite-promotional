Refunds & cancellation

Fourteen days to change your mind. Then cancel whenever you like.

No cancellation fee, no notice period, no argument about the last month. This page is the whole policy, and it forms part of the Terms of Service.

The 14 days

1

The first payment is refundable in full.

Whatever plan you started on and however well the demo went, you have 14 days from your first payment to ask for it back — the whole amount, not a credit note. You do not have to explain why, and we will not try to talk you out of it.

  • How to ask: a message on WhatsApp or email from the account owner, within 14 days of the payment. Any wording will do.
  • How it is paid: back to the method you paid with, normally within five working days of the request.
  • What happens to your data: if you want a copy, we take the export and export the database for you first. If you would rather it were simply gone, on a hosted install we delete it; on a self-hosted install it is already on your machine and yours to remove. The choice is yours and we do not need a reason.

If a payment in a later period was made in error — a duplicate invoice, a card charged twice — that is refunded in full whenever it comes up, not only inside the 14 days.

Monthly plans

2

Cancel any time. Keep what you paid for.

  • After the 14 days, a monthly plan is rolling. Tell us before your renewal date and you are not billed again.
  • Your access runs to the last day you have paid for, even if you cancel on the first day of that month. Nothing is switched off mid-month.
  • There is no pro-rata refund of a month that has already started, and no cancellation fee.
  • Ask us to cancel on the day you decide and we will confirm the exact date your access ends, in writing.

Annual plans

3

Unused whole months come back.

If you pay for a year and leave during it, the refund is calculated on the months you did not use. The month you are in when you cancel is not refunded; every whole month after it is.

Worked example: you pay 1,299 for a year and cancel 4 months in, with 8 months left. If the month you are in is not yet complete, 7 whole months are refunded — 1,299 ÷ 12 × 7 = 758 QAR. The arithmetic is done at the plan price you paid, never at the higher monthly rate, and we show it to you before we process it.

Cancelling inside the first 14 days is simpler: it is the full-refund case above, whatever the plan.

What is not refunded

4

The short list, so there is no surprise.

  • Work already delivered under a separate quote — loading your services and staff, training your team, or custom development. If we did not deliver what the quote said, we do it again, and if we cannot, that part is refunded.
  • Costs already spent on your behalf through a third party — a hosting plan, a certificate or a domain bought in your name. We tell you before anything like this is purchased, and it stays yours either way.
  • A period that was used and has ended. Refunds look forward, not back.

Nothing on this page is intended to reduce a right you have under Qatari law, and if something here ever conflicts with it, the law wins.

How to cancel

5

One message, from the right person.

  • Send it on WhatsApp or email from the account owner’s name, or from an address we already have on your account.
  • We reply with a written confirmation: the last day your access runs, the amount refunded if any, and how it is being returned.
  • Before the switch-off we hand you the exports and a full database backup, and confirm you have them.
  • If you have not had a reply within one working day, send it again and say so — a message that never arrived is the only way a cancellation goes wrong here.

Demos

6

Looking costs nothing.

The demo is free, takes about fifteen minutes, and needs no card details and no commitment. We load your own services and staff into a copy so you see your business on screen rather than ours. If it is not right for you, saying so on the call is the end of it — there is no follow-up campaign.

That is deliberate: it is why the refund policy can be this simple. We would rather you decided before paying than changed your mind afterwards.

Cancel or ask

One message and it is done.

Whether you want to cancel, or want the arithmetic above checked before you do, WhatsApp is answered by the same person either way.
