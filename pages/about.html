About Spa Suite

We built the management platform that spas, salons, and wellness centers in Qatar actually need.

Our Story

Spa Suite was born from a simple observation: the spa and salon industry in the Gulf was growing fast, but the technology supporting these businesses was stuck in the past. Owners were juggling five or more separate tools — a booking app here, a POS system there, spreadsheets for finances, paper records for staff — and still had no clear picture of their business.

We set out to build the platform that we wished existed: one that handles everything from the moment a walk-in customer enters the door to the end-of-day financial close, with real-time visibility at every step.

Today, Spa Suite powers spas, salons, and wellness centers across Qatar, helping owners take control of their operations, delight their customers, and grow their businesses with confidence.
