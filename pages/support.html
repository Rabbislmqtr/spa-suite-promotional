Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What devices does Spa Suite work on?
Spa Suite works on any device with a modern web browser — desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones. There's nothing to install. Just open your browser and log in. We recommend using Chrome or Safari for the best experience.
Do I need internet access to use it?
Yes, Spa Suite is a web-based application and requires an internet connection. However, it's very lightweight and works well even on slow connections. Your data is securely stored in the cloud, so you can access it from any device, anywhere.
Is there a free trial?
Yes! We offer a free demo where we walk you through the entire system. You can see exactly how it works for your specific business before making any commitment. Contact us on WhatsApp to schedule your demo.
Can I have multiple staff members log in at the same time?
Absolutely. Spa Suite supports multiple simultaneous users with role-based access control. Your receptionist can use the POS, your manager can view reports, and your staff can clock in — all at the same time from different devices.
How does the multi-branch feature work?
With multi-branch support, you can manage unlimited locations from one account. Each branch has its own cash register, staff, and room management, while sharing a unified customer database and loyalty program. Admins can switch between branches or view consolidated reports across all locations.
Is my data safe and secure?
Security is our top priority. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We perform automatic daily backups, and you can create manual backups anytime with one click. Role-based access ensures staff only see what they're authorized to see. Full audit trails track every important action.
Do you offer training for my team?
Yes, we provide hands-on training during setup. We'll walk your team through every feature they need — from using the POS to clocking in to viewing reports. Ongoing support is available via WhatsApp, email, or phone whenever you need it.
Can I export my data?
Yes. You can export sales reports, customer lists, financial statements, and feedback data as spreadsheets or PDFs. You also have full database backup capabilities, so you always have complete access to your data.
